<design>
`ΔLIST` is U+0394, `ΣLIST` and `Σ` are U+03A3, and `ΠLIST` -- filed under
`list` and written in plan 01 -- is U+03A0. They print identically on a
Windows console, which is why every probe for them goes through a script with
the encoding pinned rather than through a command line. The three LIST
functions are one family in two groups, and each entry links to the others so
that a reader who finds one finds all three.

The interpreter implements four of these 25: EXP, INVERSE, LN and LOG. They
are drafted first; the rest wait for the batch.
</design>
