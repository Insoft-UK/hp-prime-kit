<design>
`SERIAL` answers the calculator's own serial number, and `results.tsv` is
committed and now published. The guard built in plan 01 keeps the answer out
of the file and out of the report; this plan is the first to depend on it, and
the first task is to confirm it works before the batch runs rather than after.

`VERSION` is the neighbour worth reading next to it: the kit already keeps
only its version number and build date, because the same leak happened
through it once.

`UTPC`, `UTPF`, `UTPN` and `UTPT` are upper-tail probabilities that HP files
in `catalog` rather than in `probability`. The inventory is followed, and each
links to the distribution it belongs with in plan 03's group.
</design>
