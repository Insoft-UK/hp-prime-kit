<design>
The six trigonometry functions -- ACOT ACSC ASEC COT CSC SEC -- answer
different numbers in degrees and in radians, and the batch runs in whatever
mode the throwaway calculator is in. That mode is part of the measurement:
the entry states the answer and the mode it was measured in, and does not
pretend the number is mode-independent. The same applies to the inverse
functions.

`units` is the group that touches `ppl.type-codes`: a `TEVAL` answer already
came back as type 9, which HP's help calls a unit, and this is the group that
can confirm or contradict it. `UVAL` and `UPART` take a value apart, so
`UVAL(2_m)` and `UPART(2_m)` measured together say what the two halves are.
</design>
