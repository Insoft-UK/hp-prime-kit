<design>
Five of them -- RANDOM, RANDINT, RANDNORM, RANDSEED and the random half of
GEOMETRIC -- answer something different every time. An answer that cannot
repeat is not an example: `results.tsv` would hold a number no later run
reproduces, and the checker would report a disagreement every time somebody
re-ran the batch. Those entries state the shape and the range of the answer,
labelled from the run that was seen, and say why there is no example to
compare against. `RANDSEED` is the one that makes the others repeatable, and
the pair is worth measuring together: seed, then draw, then seed again and
draw again.

The three families -- density, cumulative, inverse -- come in sets of seven
(`NORMALD`, `NORMALD_CDF`, `NORMALD_ICDF` and the same for CHISQUARE, FISHER,
POISSON, STUDENT, BINOMIAL, GEOMETRIC). An entry for one of a family says
what the family shares once and links, rather than repeating it 21 times.
</design>
