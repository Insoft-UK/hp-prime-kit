Shaped as batch 1, with two changes learned from it:

- **An effect row catches its own failure** with an `IFERR` inside the body,
  so the setting is put back even when the measurement is refused. In batch 1
  a refused measurement would have left the setting changed for every later
  row.
- **The rows that delete come after everything that reads**: `DelAVars` and
  `DelAFiles`, each followed by a second read of what they delete.

The questions: `8 / 2 MOD 3` and `9 MOD 4 / 2`, where the two groupings give
different answers; `2 ^ 3 NTHROOT 8`, `-3 NTHROOT 8` and a chain of two
`NTHROOT`; an empty list and a string indexed at 0, and a string at 2;
`R→B(255)` under `Base` 0, 1 and 2; and, last, `2 NTHROOT (-4)` and
`3 NTHROOT (-8)` with `HComplex` at 1.
