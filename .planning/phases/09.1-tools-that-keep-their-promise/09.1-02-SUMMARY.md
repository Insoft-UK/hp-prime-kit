# Plan 02 summary: a batch never loses or replaces evidence in silence

## What was built

- **The two infinities decode.** Sign nibble 2 with exponent 499 and twelve
  nines is negative infinity, 6 is positive infinity, as the emulator wrote
  them on 2026-09-13. Anything wider is still refused: another nibble, and 2
  with exponent 498 or another mantissa, are tested. A new fact holds it,
  [formats.number-infinity](../../../docs/topics/formats.md#formats.number-infinity),
  labelled `emulator`, with the bytes of both.
- **One unreadable cell costs one row.** `read_hpmat(strict=False)` gives
  None for a cell it cannot decode. A row whose number alone is unreadable is
  kept from its text; any other unreadable cell makes the row `UNREADABLE`,
  reported and not stored, and the rest of the batch is written.
- **A stored row is not replaced by a different answer.** The report shows
  `KEPT` and the stored answer, the command exits 1, and
  `--collect --replace` lets the new one in. The same answer again is
  written, with its new date.
- **A probe on a call that already has a row is refused before the emulator
  opens**, unless `--replace` is given. The check comes before anything else
  the run looks at.
- **A row carries the day `M9.hpmat` was written**, which is when the
  emulator closed after the run. The date is no longer kept in the saved
  state. Stored rows keep theirs.
- **Tests**: `test_numbers.py` from 60 to 67, `test_examples_run.py` from 47
  to 56. `tools.md` says what `--replace` does, what `KEPT` and
  `UNREADABLE` mean, and what a row's date is.

## Deviations

- **The infinities became a fact of their own**, not a paragraph of
  `formats.number`: that fact is labelled `G2`, from the Rosetta stone, and
  the infinities were measured on the emulator. The README's count moved to
  118 facts and 20 `emulator`, and its test held it until it did.
- **A different answer exits 1.** The plan said it is reported; a run that
  keeps a stored row has something for a person to settle, so a script
  should see it.
