<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: A statement ends where the calculator says it ends</name>
  <files>hpkit/interp.py, tests/test_interp.py</files>
  <action>After a statement, the parser requires `;`, the keyword that closes
  its block (`END`, `ELSE`, `UNTIL`, `THEN` in an `IFERR`, `DEFAULT`), or the
  end of the file. Anything else raises Unsupported, naming the word and its
  line. Tests: an unknown word after an expression raises in `RETURN` and in
  an assignment; the body of every existing test and example still loads.</action>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: MOD and NTHROOT as the calculator writes them</name>
  <files>hpkit/interp.py, tests/test_interp.py</files>
  <action>`MOD` is written between its operands and computes the Euclidean
  remainder the builtin already has; `NTHROOT` takes the degree on its left
  and the number on its right. How either binds against `+`, `-`, `*` and `/`
  has not been measured, so an unparenthesised one mixed with those raises
  not covered and says to add parentheses; against a comparison or a logical
  operator it binds tighter, as arithmetic does, which the entry labels
  unverified. The call forms raise PPLError with the calculator's refusal:
  written between its operands, not as a call. Tests: `9 MOD 4` 1,
  `(9 MOD 4) + 100` 101, `9 MOD 4 + 100` refused, `3 NTHROOT 8` 2,
  `n MOD 2 == 0` true for 4, `MOD(9,4)` and `NTHROOT(3,8)` refused. The
  binding goes on Phase 8.1's list of open questions.</action>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 3: A builtin that cannot handle its arguments says so</name>
  <files>hpkit/interp.py, tests/test_interp.py</files>
  <action>Where builtins are called, a TypeError, ValueError, IndexError or
  AttributeError from inside one becomes Unsupported: the builtin's name and
  the kinds of its arguments. Tests: `CONCAT({1,2,3},4)` and `FLOOR({3.2})`
  raise Unsupported, not TypeError; `hpprime run` prints one line and exits
  1, with no traceback.</action>
</task>
