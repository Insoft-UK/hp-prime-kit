<format>
The format, as the plan fixes it (docs/format.md states it for readers):

Command entry, `docs/commands/<group>/<NAME>.md`:
1. `# NAME`, equal to the file name.
2. One sentence: what it does.
3. A two-column fields table with rows `Syntax` (one row per form, with what it
   returns), `Group` (equal to the folder), `Runs on the PC` (`yes` or `no`).
4. `## Examples`: a table `Call | Result | Known from`. Call and result are PPL
   in backticks. A call with a `;` in it is a function body that ends in
   `RETURN`; otherwise it is an expression. The result is written as the
   calculator displays it. Known from is exactly one label, optionally a link
   to its evidence.
5. `## Behaviour`: paragraphs; every paragraph carries at least one label in
   parentheses.
6. `## Models get wrong` (optional): a table `They write | What happens | Seen in`.
   Only with a record of where it was seen.
7. `## Related` (optional): links to other entries or facts.

Labels: `G2`, `emulator`, `HP help`, `unverified`, in that order of strength.
`HP help` means Command Tree 13217 unless the entry says otherwise.

Fact, in `docs/topics/<topic>.md`: `<a name="topic.slug"></a>` followed by a
`##` heading that states it; a fields table with `Identifier` (the same
identifier), `Kind` (`rule` or `refuted hypothesis`) and `Known from` (one
label); a paragraph that states it; and a paragraph that starts **Evidence.**
</format>
