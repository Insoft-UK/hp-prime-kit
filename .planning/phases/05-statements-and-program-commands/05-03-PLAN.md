</objective>

<blocked>
Measured 2026-09-12, before writing anything: the interpreter implements
none of these 26 -- `%CHANGE` and `%TOTAL` do not even parse -- and HP's
help dump holds no example with a result for any of them. So not one entry
can be written without inventing its Result column, and the whole plan waits
for a batch, as the integer group did.

The batch runs the calls first; the entries are written from what it
answers. That is the order the last two plans arrived at the hard way, and
it is the order this one starts in.
</blocked>
