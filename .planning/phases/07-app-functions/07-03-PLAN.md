<design>
**These four families go first because everything else takes their objects as
arguments.** A curve wants points, a transform wants an object, a test wants
two lines. Measuring the constructors first means the later batches can be
written against shapes already proven rather than guessed.

The probe measured eight of them and they answer from Home with no app open:
`point(3,4)` comes back as itself, `distance` of the origin and 3,4 is 5,
`slope` is `1/2` exact rather than decimal, `midpoint` of a segment is
`point(1,1)`, and `area` of a circle through the origin and 1,0 is a quarter
of pi -- which also settled that `circle` takes its two points as a diameter.

**Every call carries an answer that arithmetic knows in advance where one
exists.** That is what separates a command computing from a command handing
something back, and it is how the explorer four were checked in plan 02.
`angle` of three points on the axes is a right angle; `abscissa` of
`point(3,4)` is 3; `radius` of the same circle is a half; `perimeter` of a
triangle with sides 3, 4 and 5 is 12.

Every call goes inside `EXPR`, as in every batch since plan 06-05.

**`randNorm` is left out on purpose**: it has no published syntax and its
name says its answer changes every run, so it cannot have an example. Its
entry says that rather than storing one draw.
</design>
