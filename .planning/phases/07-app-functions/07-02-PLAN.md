<design>
**Three shapes live in `spreadsheet` and they are measured differently.**
`AVERAGE`, `SUM` and the `Conf`/`Hyp` families take their data as an argument
and can be measured like any other function. `AMORT`, `REGRS`, `STAT1`,
`STAT2`, `PredX` and `PredY` take a range or a model, which is a shape this
kit has not sent before. `CellHasData` and `ClearCell` take nothing at all and
read the cell the user is standing on.

**That last pair is the interesting one and it is measured, not reasoned
about.** A batch runs a program from Home on a calculator reset before the
run, so there is no current cell. What they answer there -- an error, a zero,
or something else -- is the row, and the entry says plainly that it is the
answer with no cell rather than the answer in general. Phase 6 learned this
from `CopyVar`, whose row measured that the call was accepted and not that it
copied, and said so.

**The explorer four are a free cross-check.** `QuadSolve(1,-3,2)` has roots 1
and 2, and `QuadDelta(1,-3,2)` is 1, both known before the calculator is
asked. A row that disagrees with arithmetic is worth more than a row that
merely arrives.

Every call goes inside `EXPR`, as in plan 01.
</design>
